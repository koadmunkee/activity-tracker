Activity Tracker

Activity Tracker is a personal health tracking application designed to work with data from Google Health API v4 to both (1) track meal data and (2) provide health insights through flexible reporting in DataStudio.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This application accesses Google Health API data solely for individual analysis. We do not sell, transfer, or share user data with third parties or advertising platforms.
